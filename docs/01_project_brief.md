# Starship Protocol — Project Brief
Rayyan Shah | BDI310 | 5 November 2026

## Concept
Starship Protocol is a small interactive science-fiction signal console. A visitor acts as a ship communications officer: holding a physical button transmits a signal, turns on an LED, and changes a browser console from STANDBY to SIGNAL ACTIVE. Releasing the button ends transmission. A counter records how many separate transmissions were started.

## Purpose and audience
Make the relationship between physical input, microcontroller logic, and digital feedback easy to understand. The audience is classmates and visitors trying a short classroom demonstration, with no account or instructions beyond a brief on-screen prompt.

## Minimum complete experience
One physical button, one LED, one ESP32, and one responsive console page. Include an explicitly labelled offline simulation for inspecting the interface without a connected kit.

## Scope
No login, cloud database, paid service, multiplayer system, or genuine radio transmission. The starship terminology describes a fictional interaction. Physical hardware is required to demonstrate the real input/output loop.
